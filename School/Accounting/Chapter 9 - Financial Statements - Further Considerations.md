# 📊 Chapter 9: Financial Statements - Further Considerations

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope & Pedagogy**: This chapter integrates advanced treatment of Carriage Inwards, Carriage Outwards, Returns Inwards, Returns Outwards, Goods for Own Use, and Inventory Valuation (lower of cost and net realizable value). It serves as the master synthesis of Chapters 1–8 for sole proprietor final accounts.

---

## 9.1 Carriage Inwards vs. Carriage Outwards

Transportation costs must be correctly classified:

$$\begin{array}{|l|l|l|}
\hline
\textbf{Freight / Carriage Item} & \textbf{Accounting Treatment} & \textbf{Impact on Financial Statements} \\ \hline
\textbf{Carriage Inwards} & \text{Added to Purchases in Trading Section} & \text{Increases COGS } \implies \text{Reduces Gross Profit} \\ \hline
\textbf{Carriage Outwards} & \text{Operating Expense in P\&L Section} & \text{Reduces Net Profit (No effect on Gross Profit)} \\ \hline
\end{array}$$

---

## 9.2 Inventory Valuation: Lower of Cost and NRV (IAS 2 / Concept)

Inventory must be valued at the **lower of cost and Net Realizable Value (NRV)**:

$$\text{NRV} = \text{Estimated Selling Price} - \text{Estimated Completion \& Selling Costs}$$

---

## 9.3 Adjustments for Goods Taken by Owner for Own Use

When the owner withdraws trading inventory for personal use:
$$\text{Journal Entry}: \quad \text{Debit: Drawings}, \quad \text{Credit: Purchases (at Cost Price)}$$
This removes the item from Purchases in the Trading Account so cost of sales is not distorted.

## 9.6 Exam Checklist & Trap Warnings

> [!EXAM-TRAP]
> **Essential Checklist**:
> 1. **Carriage Inwards** $\implies$ Added to Purchases in Trading section.
> 2. **Carriage Outwards** $\implies$ Listed under Operating Expenses in P&L section.
> 3. **Goods for Own Use** $\implies$ Deducted from Purchases in Trading section AND added to total Drawings in Capital section.
> 4. **Closing Inventory** $\implies$ Appears TWICE: subtracted in Trading section AND added as Current Asset in Statement of Financial Position.
