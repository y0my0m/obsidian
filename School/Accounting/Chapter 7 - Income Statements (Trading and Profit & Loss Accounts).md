# 📈 Chapter 7: Income Statements (Trading and Profit & Loss Accounts)

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope & Pedagogy**: This chapter covers the preparation of the Income Statement for a trading business. It explains the calculation of Gross Profit in the Trading Account section and Net Profit in the Profit & Loss section. It excludes balance sheet classifications and complex year-end adjustments, covered in Chapters 8 and 9.

---

## 7.1 Structure & Purpose of the Income Statement

An **Income Statement** measures business financial performance over an accounting period. It consists of two distinct sections:

1. **The Trading Account**: Calculates **Gross Profit** from buying and selling goods.
2. **The Profit & Loss Account**: Calculates **Net Profit** by adding other income and deducting operating expenses.

---

## 7.2 The Trading Account Section (Gross Profit Mechanics)

$$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Sales Revenue} - \text{Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)}$$

### Key Trading Formulas:
1. **Net Sales Revenue**:
   $$\text{Net Sales} = \text{Gross Sales Revenue} - \text{Returns Inwards}$$

2. **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)**:
   $$\text{COGS} = \text{Opening Inventory} + \text{Net Purchases} - \text{Closing Inventory}$$
   $$\text{Net Purchases} = \text{Gross Purchases} - \text{Returns Outwards}$$

---

## 7.3 The Profit & Loss Account Section (Net Profit Mechanics)

$$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Gross Profit} + \text{Other Income} - \text{Total Operating Expenses}$$

- **Other Income**: Rent Received, Discounts Received, Commission Received.
- **Operating Expenses**: Rent, Rates, Salaries & Wages, Insurance, Electricity, Advertising, Discounts Allowed.

---

## 7.5 Closing Transfers to the Income Statement

At year-end, nominal account balances are closed off by transferring them to the Income Statement via journal entries:

1. **Transferring Purchases & Expense Balances**:
   - Debit: Income Statement, Credit: Purchases / Expense Accounts.
2. **Transferring Sales & Income Balances**:
   - Debit: Sales / Income Accounts, Credit: Income Statement.
3. **Transferring Net Profit to Capital**:
   - Debit: Income Statement, Credit: Capital Account.

---

## 7.6 Key Takeaways

> [!KEY-CONCEPT]
> - **Gross Profit**: Excess of net sales over cost of goods sold.
> - **Net Profit**: Final profit remaining after deducting all operating expenses.
