# 🏦 Chapter 8: Statements of Financial Position (Balance Sheets)

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope & Pedagogy**: This chapter covers the classification and presentation of assets, liabilities, and capital in a Statement of Financial Position. It explains asset/liability order of permanence and liquidity, and the Working Capital formula. It excludes complex adjustments covered in Chapter 9.

---

## 8.1 Nature & Purpose of the Statement of Financial Position

A **Statement of Financial Position** is a structured financial statement showing the assets, liabilities, and owner's capital of an entity on a specific date.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Key Distinction**:
> The Statement of Financial Position is **NOT** part of the double-entry ledger system. It is a structured report listing open balances remaining on the Trial Balance after preparing the Income Statement.

---

## 8.2 Asset and Liability Classifications

### 1. Non-Current Assets
Long-term resources acquired for continuing use in operations rather than resale, expected to generate benefits for over one year (e.g., Land & Buildings, Machinery, Motor Vehicles, Fixtures & Fittings). Listed in order of **permanence**.

### 2. Current Assets
Short-term assets held for conversion into cash within one year (e.g., Inventory, Trade Receivables, Bank, Cash). Listed in order of **reverse liquidity**:
$$\text{Inventory} \longrightarrow \text{Trade Receivables} \longrightarrow \text{Bank} \longrightarrow \text{Cash}$$

### 3. Current Liabilities
Short-term obligations due for settlement within one year (e.g., Trade Payables, Bank Overdraft).

### 4. Non-Current Liabilities
Long-term obligations payable after one year (e.g., Bank Loans, Mortgages).

---

## 8.3 Working Capital & Net Assets Formulas

$$\text{Working Capital} = \text{Current Assets} - \text{Current Liabilities}$$

$$\text{Net Assets} = \text{Total Assets} - \text{Total Liabilities}$$

$$\text{Capital Employed} = \text{Opening Capital} + \text{Net Profit} - \text{Drawings}$$

---

\text{Trade Receivables} & 6,800 & & \\
\text{Cash at Bank} & 15,100 & & \\
\text{Cash in Hand} & 200 & 34,100 & \\ \hline
\text{Less: Current Liabilities} & & & \\
\quad \text{Trade Payables} & & (9,100) & \\ \hline
\textbf{Net Current Assets (Working Capital)} & & & \mathbf{25,000} \\ \hline
\textbf{Total Assets Less Current Liabilities} & & & \mathbf{83,000} \\
\text{Less: Non-Current Liabilities} & & & \\
\quad \text{Bank Loan (5-year)} & & & (10,000) \\ \hline
\textbf{NET ASSETS} & & & \mathbf{73,000} \\ \hline\hline
\textbf{CAPITAL} & & & \\
\text{Opening Capital} & & 60,000 & \\

& & 80,500 & \\
\text{Less: Drawings} & & (7,500) & \\ \hline
\textbf{TOTAL CAPITAL} & & & \mathbf{73,000} \\ \hline\hline
\end{array}$$

---

## 8.5 Key Summary

> [!KEY-CONCEPT]
> $$\text{Net Assets} = \text{Total Capital}$$
> Both sides of the vertical statement must equal $\$73,000$.
