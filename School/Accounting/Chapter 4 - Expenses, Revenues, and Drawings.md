# 💸 Chapter 4: Expenses, Revenues, and Drawings

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope & Pedagogy**: This chapter extends double-entry bookkeeping to cover operating expenses, revenues (service income), and owner's drawings. It explains how profit and loss impact owner's capital. It precedes account balancing and trial balance extraction, covered in Chapters 5 and 6.

---

## 4.1 Profit, Revenues, and Expenses

Trading operations generate revenues and incur operating expenses:
- **Revenue (Income)**: The gross inflow of economic benefits arising from the ordinary activities of a business (e.g., Sales, Rent Received, Commission Received).
- **Expenses**: Costs incurred in running the business and generating revenues (e.g., Rent, Wages, Electricity, Insurance, Motor Expenses).

### The Profit Equation
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Revenues} - \text{Expenses}$$
If Expenses exceed Revenues, the business incurs a **Net Loss**.

---

## 4.2 Impact of Profit and Drawings on Capital

Profit increases the net assets of the business, belonging to the owner. Drawings represent withdrawals of cash or goods by the owner for private use.

$$\text{Expanded Accounting Equation}:$$
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Capital} + (\text{Revenues} - \text{Expenses}) - \text{Drawings} + \text{Liabilities}$$

---

## 4.3 Double-Entry Rules for Nominal Accounts (Expenses & Revenues)

| **Account Type** | **To Record an INCREASE (+)** | **To Record a DECREASE (-)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Expense Accounts** | DEBIT (Dr) | CREDIT (Cr) |
| **Revenue Accounts** | CREDIT (Cr) | DEBIT (Dr) |
| **Drawings Account** | DEBIT (Dr) | CREDIT (Cr) |

### Summary Classification Matrix (7 Element Rules)

| **DEBIT (Dr) Increases** | **CREDIT (Cr) Increases** |
| --- | --- |
| Assets | Liabilities |
| Expenses | Revenues / Income |
| Drawings / Losses | Capital / Profits |

---

## 4.4 Handling Owner's Drawings

When the owner takes cash or goods from the business for personal use:

1. **Cash Drawings**:
   - Debit: **Drawings Account**
   - Credit: **Cash / Bank Account**

2. **Goods Taken for Own Use**:
   - Debit: **Drawings Account**
   - Credit: **Purchases Account** *(at cost price, to remove the cost from goods bought for resale)*

---

## 4.6 Key Takeaways

> [!KEY-CONCEPT]
> - Expenses always carry **Debit** balances.
> - Revenues always carry **Credit** balances.
> - Drawings always carry a **Debit** balance and reduce net owner's equity.
