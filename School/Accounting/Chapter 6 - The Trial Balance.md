# 🧮 Chapter 6: The Trial Balance

> [!NOTE]
> **Scope & Pedagogy**: This chapter explains the preparation, structure, and limitations of a Trial Balance. It teaches how to extract balances from ledger accounts to test arithmetical accuracy. It precedes final accounts preparation in Chapters 7, 8, and 9.

---

## 6.1 Definition & Purpose of a Trial Balance

A **Trial Balance** is a statement listing the debit and credit balances extracted from all ledger accounts on a specific date.

### Primary Objectives:
1. To test the **arithmetical accuracy** of double-entry postings.
2. To assist in locating bookkeeping errors.
3. To provide a consolidated summary of account balances for preparing financial statements.

---

## 6.2 Mathematical Basis of the Trial Balance

Because every transaction is posted as equal debits and credits ($\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$), the total of all debit balances in the ledger must equal the total of all credit balances:

$$\sum \text{Debit Balances} = \sum \text{Credit Balances}$$

---

## 6.3 Classification Rules for the Trial Balance

To extract a trial balance correctly, recall the **PEARLS / PEAR vs CLIP** mnemonic:

| **DEBIT (Dr) Column Balances** | **CREDIT (Cr) Column Balances** |
| --- | --- |
| **P**urchases | **C**apital |
| **E**xpenses | **L**iabilities (Trade Payables, Loans) |
| **A**ssets (Premises, Van, Cash, Bank) | **I**ncome / Revenues (Sales, Rent Rec.) |
| **R**eturns Inwards (Sales Returns) | **P**urchases Returns (Returns Outwards) |
| **S**et-up Drawings |  |

---

## 6.4 Errors That Do NOT Affect Trial Balance Agreement

Even when total debits equal total credits, the books may still contain errors. Six classic errors will **not** disrupt trial balance balance:

1. **Error of Omission**: A complete transaction is entirely omitted from the books.
2. **Error of Commission**: An entry is posted to the correct side of the wrong personal account (e.g., debited to C. Smith instead of H. Smith).
3. **Error of Principle**: An entry is posted to the wrong class of account in violation of accounting principles (e.g., buying a motor van debited to Motor Expenses).
4. **Error of Original Entry**: An incorrect figure is entered in a book of prime entry and posted to both ledger accounts (e.g., $\$150$ entered as $\$510$).
5. **Compensating Errors**: Two independent errors cancel each other out mathematically.
6. **Complete Reversal of Entries**: The correct accounts are used but debited/credited in reverse.

## 6.5 Worked Example: Extracting a Trial Balance

### List of Ledger Balances at 31 May 2020:
- Purchases: $\$994$
- Sales: $\$490$
- Returns Outwards: $\$15$
- Returns Inwards: $\$1$
- Trade Payable (A. Lyon): $\$2$
- Trade Receivable (M. Spencer): $\$29$
- Cash at Bank: $\$90$
- Capital: $\$607$

### Trial Balance Format

## 6.6 Key Summary

> [!EXAM-TRAP]
> - Opening Inventory **is** included in the Trial Balance.
> - Closing Inventory **is not** included in the trial balance (it is valued at year-end after stocktaking).
